The left table gives the seven parameters the paper tuned, with their BSIM4 meaning. The right table groups the 45 parameters of the screened set by the part of the model they act on. Meanings of the other 38: TOXE electrical oxide thickness; TOXM oxide thickness at which parameters were extracted; XJ source/drain junction depth; NDEP channel doping at the depletion edge; NSD source/drain doping; DWG gate-bias dependence of effective width; VFB flat-band voltage; K1, K2 first- and second-order body-effect coefficients; K3 narrow-width coefficient; K3B body dependence of K3; W0 narrow-width parameter; LPE0, LPEB lateral non-uniform doping and its body dependence; DVT0, DVT1 short-channel coefficients of Vth; DVT0W, DVT1W narrow-width coefficients of Vth at small L; UA, UB first- and second-order mobility degradation by the vertical field; A0 channel-length dependence of the bulk charge effect; A2 second non-saturation factor; AGS gate-bias coefficient of the bulk charge factor; B0, B1 width coefficient and width offset of the bulk charge effect; PRWG gate-bias dependence of the series resistance; WR width offset for the series resistance; RSH source/drain sheet resistance; VOFF subthreshold offset voltage; CIT interface trap capacitance; CDSC source/drain-to-channel coupling capacitance; DSUB DIBL exponent in subthreshold; PCLM channel length modulation; PDIBLC1, PDIBLC2 first and second DIBL corrections of the output resistance; DROUT length dependence of that correction; ALPHA0, BETA0 impact ionization current parameters. The box is in log space, so a parameter with value zero in a device's bin is not fitted on that device: 39 to 42 of the 45 are fitted per device.
