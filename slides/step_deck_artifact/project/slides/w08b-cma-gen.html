CMA-ES: one generation

  • CMA-ES keeps a mean m, a step size σ and a covariance C of the scaled parameters (-1 to 1).
  • 1. Sample λ = 4 + ⌊3 ln n⌋ cards x = m + σ·N(0, C); cma's BoundTransform maps each into [-1, 1].
  • 2. Run NGSpice for each card and compute its device RRMS; a curve NGSpice cannot simulate counts as 10.
  • 3. Sort the λ cards by device RRMS; the update uses the ranks, not the RRMS values.
  • 4. New m = weighted mean of the μ = ⌊λ/2⌋ lowest-RRMS cards; the weight decreases with rank.
  • 5. C is updated from the steps of the μ lowest-RRMS cards and the evolution path; the other cards enter with negative weights (active CMA).
  • 6. σ increases when the evolution path (sum of recent mean steps) is longer than for random steps, and decreases when it is shorter.
  • Stop after 3,000 / 8,000 evaluations (7 / 45 parameters) or at cma's own stop criteria; cma 4.4.4.
  • Figure: VTH0 and U0 free, other parameters at the round-1 card of start 2; λ = 6, μ = 3; lowest device RRMS 0.961 in generation 1, 0.163 after 101 generations (606 runs).
The figure is a separate run with only VTH0 and U0 free; the other parameters are fixed at the round-1 card of start 2, and the grey level is the device RRMS on a 30 x 30 grid of NGSpice runs. The 40-parameter run of the example device applies the same update to 40 coordinates with λ = 15 and μ = 7.
