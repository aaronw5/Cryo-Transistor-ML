Step D: penalty and lambda

  • gm / I = d ln I / d|Vgs|, gds / I = d ln I / d|Vds| (1/V); P is the mean negative part of both
  • Grid: 25 mV steps, 75 x 75 points, 0-1.85 V, forward bias, I_card > 1 nA
  • lambda = penalty weight; grid {0, 1, 2, 5, 10} V
  • lambda* = 1 V, chosen by nested cross-validation over the training data (18 devices in 3 groups: train g on 2, refit the 3rd with g fixed; minimum mean ln RRMS, tie to the larger lambda)
A correction that fits the curves can still make gm or gds negative between the measured biases, which a circuit simulator does not accept. The penalty P measures the negative part of both log-slopes of the corrected current on a dense bias grid. lambda sets its weight in the loss. lambda is chosen from five values by cross-validation over the 18 devices' data, and the chosen value is 1 V.
