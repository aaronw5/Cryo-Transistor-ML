Step D: penalty and lambda

  • gm = dId/dVgs, gds = dId/dVds; gm / I = d ln I / d|Vgs|, gds / I = d ln I / d|Vds| (1/V); P = mean negative part of both
  • Grid: 25 mV steps, 75 x 75 points, 0-1.85 V, forward bias, I_card > 1 nA
  • lambda = penalty weight; grid {0, 1, 2, 5, 10} V
  • Selection: 18 devices in 3 groups; train g on 2 groups, refit the 3rd group's cards with g fixed; lambda* = minimum mean ln RRMS (tie: larger lambda); 15 trainings, 90 refits
  • lambda* = 5 V (7p), 5 V (45p)
A correction that fits the curves can still make gm or gds negative between the measured biases, which a circuit simulator does not accept. The penalty P measures the negative part of both log-slopes of the corrected current on a dense bias grid. lambda sets its weight in the loss. lambda is chosen from five values by three-group cross-validation over the 18 devices; the chosen value is 5 V for both arms.
