One evaluation, used by both optimizers

  • CMA-ES minimizes one number per card: the device RRMS
  • Least squares minimizes Σ r_i² over all scored points i of the device
  • r_i = w_c (I_sim,i − I_meas,i) / (den_c √(n_c C)): den_c = mean |I_meas| of curve c, n_c its points, C curves
  • w_c = 1 in pass 1; pass 2: w_c ∝ 1/√RRMS_c. With w_c = 1, Σ r_i² = mean of RRMS_c²
Every evaluation writes the parameter values into the card, runs NGSpice once for all measured biases of the device, and compares the 11 simulated curves with the measured ones. CMA-ES uses the device RRMS, a single number. Least squares uses the vector of point residuals; with unit weights its sum of squares is the mean of the squared curve RRMS. Least squares runs two passes; the second weights each curve by one over the square root of its RRMS. A curve NGSpice cannot simulate is given a fixed error of 10.
