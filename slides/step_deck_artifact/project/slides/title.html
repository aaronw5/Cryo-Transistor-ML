Fitting BSIM4 parameters to SKY130 transistors at 77 K

18 measured transistors (8 nMOS, 10 pMOS), simulated in NGSpice

Compared with the paper's published 77 K card

2026-10-28

This talk shows how we fit BSIM4 model parameters to measured 77 K data of 18 SkyWater 130 nm transistors. The model and the simulator stay unchanged; only parameter values change. The result is compared with the card published in the paper.
