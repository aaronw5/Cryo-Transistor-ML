SKY130, 77 K: card fit from multiplier-1 cards, 7 and 45 parameters, with correction g

Devices: 18 (8 nMOS, 10 pMOS), measured at 77 K; simulator: NGSpice 41

Start: paper's 77 K card files with every tuned multiplier set to 1; 3 random starts per device

All-device / combined RRMS: paper card 0.2751 / 0.2595; 7-parameter card + g 0.0783 / 0.0757; 45-parameter card + g 0.0667 / 0.0635

2026-10-28 (run I25-1)

This deck covers fitting transistor model cards to measured SkyWater 130 nm I-V data at 77 K, starting from the paper's 77 K card files with every tuned value removed. There are 18 measured devices, 8 nMOS and 10 pMOS. Every number comes from NGSpice 41 runs scored with the paper's RRMS metric. All 18 devices are fitted. Two parameter sets are fitted: the paper's 7 parameters and a 45-parameter set from a sensitivity screen. Each card is then combined with a learned current correction g.
