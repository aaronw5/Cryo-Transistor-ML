This deck covers fitting transistor model cards to measured SkyWater 130 nm I-V data at 77 K. There are 18 measured devices, 8 nMOS and 10 pMOS. Every number comes from NGSpice 41 runs scored with the paper's RRMS metric. All 18 devices are fitted; there is no held-out set. The reference is the card published with the paper, at all-device RRMS 0.2751; the 43-parameter card fit with the correction g reaches 0.0696.
