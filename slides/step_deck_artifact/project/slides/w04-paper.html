The paper's card: 7 tuned parameters

ParameterMeaning
VTH0threshold voltage
U0low-field mobility
NFACTORsubthreshold slope factor
VSATsaturation velocity
DELTAlinear-to-saturation smoothing
RDSWsource/drain series resistance
ETA0threshold shift with drain voltage
  • The paper changed these 7 parameters per device; all others unchanged

  • All 18 devices: all-device RRMS 0.2751

The paper's card changes seven BSIM4 parameters for each device and leaves the rest of the card file unchanged. The figure shows the paper card on the pMOS that the step slides use as the example. Over the 18 devices the paper card has an all-device RRMS of 0.2751. Our first run fits the same seven parameters.
