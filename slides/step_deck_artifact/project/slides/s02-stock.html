Stock card and multiplier-1 card at 77 K

  • Orange: stock sky130A tt card, fitted at 300 K (tnom 30 C), run at 77 K; 18 devices: RRMS 3.418 all-device, 3.108 combined (nMOS / pMOS mean 0.318 / 5.897)
  • Purple: paper's 77 K card files (18 custom bins, tnom -196.15 C, temperature coefficients 0) with every tuned multiplier = 1; this is the fit start
  • Multiplier-1 card, 18 devices: 12.101 all-device, 10.959 combined (nMOS / pMOS mean 0.685 / 21.234)
  • Device in figure: pMOS L 0.35 x W 5 um (L, W = channel length, width in um)
The stock SkyWater card was fitted at room temperature. Run at 77 K it misses the measured pMOS currents by large factors; the pMOS mean RRMS is 5.9. The purple curves use the paper's 77 K card files, which set the nominal temperature to 77 K and the temperature coefficients to zero, with every tuned multiplier set back to 1. No tuned value from the paper is left in this card. It scores 12.1 all-device, and it is the card the fit starts from.
