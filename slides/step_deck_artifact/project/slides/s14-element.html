Verilog-A element

  • I_card = card transistor current; I_ch = channel current of a card-transistor copy
  • Verilog-A = language for analog device models; compiled with OpenVAF 23.5, loaded through OSDI (NGSpice 41 compiled-model interface)
  • Checks (next slide): I17-1 rules on the 7p and 45p card + g of this deck (g: lambda 5 V)
  • RRMS with the element in NGSpice, all-device / combined: 7p + g 0.0783 / 0.0757, 45p + g 0.0667 / 0.0635; max per-device difference to card x exp(g): 3.4e-05
To use g in circuit simulation, it is written as a Verilog-A element that adds (exp(g) - 1) times the channel current to the card transistor. The element is compiled with OpenVAF and loaded into NGSpice 41. With the element in the NGSpice deck, the RRMS of the 7-parameter and 45-parameter cards with g equals the RRMS of the card current times exp(g) to within the stated per-device difference.
