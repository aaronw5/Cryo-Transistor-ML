This slide shows why a correction on top of the card is needed. On all ten pMOS devices the fitted card gives too much current in the linear region at very low drain voltage. The fitted card leaves this excess on all ten devices. The correction g removes most of it, and this one curve family carries more than half of the RRMS reduction that g gives. The earlier g_why analysis on the previous cards found the same pattern: the measured pMOS conductance rises with drain voltage at the start of the Id-Vd curve, which the BSIM4 card does not produce.
