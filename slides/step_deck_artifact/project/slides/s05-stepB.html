Step B: remaining error after the card fit

  • Device: pMOS L 0.35 x W 5 um; 45-parameter card fit, no g; device RRMS 0.1249
  • Top: (I_card - I_meas) / den_c, den_c = mean |I_meas| of curve c (the RRMS error term); bottom: ln(I_meas / I_card) = the target of the correction g
After the card fit the error left on each curve has a smooth shape in voltage. The top row shows the error in the units RRMS uses. The bottom row shows the log ratio of measured to card current, which is the quantity the correction g is trained to reproduce. For this pMOS the largest errors are at low drain voltage: the Id-Vg curve at |Vds| 0.01 V and the start of the Id-Vd curves.
