Step 4: local refinement (least squares)

Input: card from step 3 and the range

Does: slopes from small changes of each parameter; steps that lower the sum of squared residuals; 2 passes

Output: refined card after 160 / 480 runs (7 / 45 parameters)

  • Left: device RRMS per iteration; 1 iteration = 40 + 2 runs
  • Right: start 2, each curve before (grey) and after (blue)
Least squares refines the card from the global search. Each iteration runs NGSpice once at the current card and once with each free parameter changed by a small step, which gives the slope matrix, and then tries a step; that is the number of free parameters plus two runs. Some curves go down and some go up, because the method lowers the summed squared error over all points. Start 2 goes from 0.169 to 0.163 in round 1.
