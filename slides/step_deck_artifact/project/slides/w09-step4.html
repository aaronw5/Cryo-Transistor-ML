Step 4: least-squares fit with TRF and finite-difference slopes

Inputcard after CMA-ES; range
Outputcard with the lowest device RRMS seen
  • The residual of a point is (simulated - measured current) divided by the mean measured current of its curve.
  • The loss is the sum of squared residuals. NGSpice reruns the card with each of the 40 parameters shifted by 1/200 of its log range to give the Jacobian.
  • TRF solves the linearized problem inside a trust region and tries the step; kept if the loss drops.
  • TRF runs in two passes; pass 2 weights each curve by 1/√(curve RRMS). For start 2 in round 1 the device RRMS goes from 0.169 to 0.163 in 591 runs.
One iteration costs 40 + 2 NGSpice runs: 1 at x, 40 shifted, 1 trial step. The right plot shows each curve RRMS of start 2 after CMA-ES and after FD least squares.
