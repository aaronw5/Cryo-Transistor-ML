Transistor I-V curves

  • Measured nMOS, L 1 um x W 1.6 um (L, W = channel length, width), 77 K = liquid-nitrogen temperature
  • Id-Vd: drain current vs drain-source voltage, one curve per gate-source voltage Vgs
  • Id-Vg: Id vs Vgs, one curve per Vds; gm = dId/dVgs, gds = dId/dVds (S)
  • pMOS: voltages and currents negative; plots show |V| and |Id|
A transistor is measured by sweeping its voltages and recording the drain current Id. The left plot sweeps Vds at fixed Vgs; the right plot sweeps Vgs at fixed Vds. The two slopes drawn in red are gds and gm, which circuit simulation uses directly. pMOS devices give the same curves with negative signs, so all plots use absolute values. Later slides show Id-Vg on a log scale to show the low-current region.
