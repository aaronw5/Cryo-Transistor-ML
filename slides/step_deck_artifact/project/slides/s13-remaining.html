The right panel splits the all-device RRMS of each final card into device type and curve class. Because device RRMS is the mean of its curves and the all-device value is the mean of devices, the rows add up exactly. One curve class carries the largest part for both parameter sets: the pMOS Id-Vg at a drain voltage of 0.01 V, with 44 percent of the seven-parameter RRMS and 38 percent of the 45-parameter RRMS. Its mean curve RRMS is 1.18 and 0.73. The left panel shows this curve on one pMOS: both fitted cards give more current than measured at high gate voltage.
