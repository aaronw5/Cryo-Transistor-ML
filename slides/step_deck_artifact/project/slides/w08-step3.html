Step 3: CMA-ES, loss device RRMS

Inputstart card or previous card; range
Outputcard with the lowest device RRMS
  • CMA-ES keeps a normal distribution (mean, covariance) over the scaled parameters.
  • One generation: sample 15 cards, run NGSpice for each, compute each device RRMS.
  • New mean: weighted mean of the lowest-RRMS cards; covariance and step size updated (next slide).
  • Example, round 1, 8,010 runs per start: device RRMS 1.15, 1.15, 42.5 to 0.259, 0.169, 0.160.
