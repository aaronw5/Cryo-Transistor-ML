Step 3: global search (CMA-ES)

Input: start card (round 1) or previous card (rounds 2, 3) and the range

Does: per generation: draws 15 cards (this pMOS), runs each, moves centre and spread toward lower device RRMS

Output: lowest-RRMS card after 2,990 / 8,010 runs (7 / 45 parameters)

  • Left: each dot one card (one run); line: lowest device RRMS so far
  • Right: VTH0 and U0 drawn for start 2; later generations (yellow) cluster
The global search is CMA-ES. Each generation it draws a set of cards from a normal distribution in the scaled range, runs NGSpice for each, and moves the centre and spread of the distribution toward the cards with the lowest device RRMS. The left plot shows every card of round 1 and the lowest RRMS so far for the three starts. The right plot shows the sampled values of two parameters, which contract over the generations. Lowest RRMS after round-1 CMA-ES: 0.259, 0.169, 0.160.
