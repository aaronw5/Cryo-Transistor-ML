These are the I17-1 element checks run on the 7-parameter and 45-parameter cards with g. Under the I17-1 rule both are labeled not deployable. In the 7-parameter case the only core failure is 43 grid points on the nMOS 100 by 100 micrometer device where the element differs from the card times exp(g) by up to 1.7e-13 A, above the 1e-13 A floor of the limit. The 45-parameter case has the same kind of points (299, up to 5.7e-13 A), a tight-tolerance convergence failure on the nMOS 20 by 0.64 micrometer card that also occurs for the card without g, and a negative gm region introduced by g on the 100 by 100 device. Restrictions: default-tolerance difference, gds sign changes and run time up to 4.5 times the card.
