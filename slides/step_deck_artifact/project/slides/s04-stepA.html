Step A: card fit

  • Parameters: 7 (VTH0, U0, NFACTOR, VSAT, DELTA, RDSW, ETA0), then 43 = 7 + 36 from a 115-candidate sensitivity screen
  • Box (log): VTH0 x/÷ 3, other 6 x/÷ 100, other 36 x/÷ 3; starts: box center + 4 best of 10,000 Latin-hypercube runs
  • CMA-ES 3,000 (7p) / 8,000 (43p) evaluations, seed 20261015, then TRF; rounds 2-3: re-centered box, width x 0.5
  • 43p after FD: 0.1044; after a2 rule: 0.1109 (combined 0.1049) = starting card; 47,502 calls + 10,000 bank per device
Step A fits the card directly in NGSpice, first with the paper's seven parameters and then with 43. The seven-parameter stage reaches all-device RRMS 0.1676. Three 43-parameter rounds bring it to 0.1049, and an FD polish (TRF, max 400 calls) to 0.1044. The a2 rule then sets a2 above 1 to 1 and a1 to 0, as the BSIM4 parameter check requires; this changes 5 devices and gives 0.1109, the starting card for the next steps. One exception: pMOS L 0.35 x W 1.6 um starts from the paper values for 5 of the 7 parameters.
