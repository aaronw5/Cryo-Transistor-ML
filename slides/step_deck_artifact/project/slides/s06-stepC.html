Step C: correction g

  • I_card = NGSpice current of the device card; exp(g) = current factor, |g| < 1; g = 0 gives I = I_card
  • s = +1 (nMOS), -1 (pMOS); Vgd = Vgs - Vds; EPS = 0.01 V; VDD = 1.85 V; x1, x2 unchanged under drain-source exchange
  • h_j = MLP 3-16-16-1 (weighted sums + tanh) on is_pmos and z1, z2 = standardized x1, x2; seeds 0-4
  • D = 18 devices, Step A cards fixed; W = network weights; Adam, rate 3e-3, 2,000 steps, L2 1e-4; one g per arm
  • Then each card is refit with g fixed (TRF, max 400 calls, Step A box)
The correction multiplies the card current by exp(g), where g is a small neural network of the two bias inputs x1 and x2. The inputs are built so that swapping drain and source gives the same value, which a circuit simulator needs. g is the mean of five networks trained from seeds 0 to 4, each squashed by tanh, so the factor stays between about 0.37 and 2.7. The loss is the mean device RRMS over the 18 devices with g applied, plus weight decay and the penalty on the next slide. One g is trained per parameter arm; then each device's card is refit with that g in the loop.
